## 1. Commercial Value Proposition (Evidence-Based, Zero Claims)

- **Target Profile:** Independent dental clinics, diagnostic centers, and local healthcare practices in Indian cities (Hyderabad, Guntur, Vijayawada, Bangalore).
- **The Core Problem:** Prospective patient inquiries often arrive after clinic operating hours (8:00 PM – 8:00 AM) or during weekend closures. Response delay outside clinic hours is measured directly from reception logs during the Day 1 intake audit (OWNER_MEASURED: Reception call and chat logs audited during Day 1 intake). With reception staff off-duty, unanswered inquiries risk booking with competing practices.
- **The Genuine Solution:** A deliverable-first setup utilizing 100% free, client-owned tools (WhatsApp Business App, Google Sheets, Google Calendar / Cal.com, Google Business Profile).
- **Pricing:** `Price: TBD. Owner decides.` The owner sets their pilot fee directly with the client.
- **Client Recurring Software Cost:** Zero recurring software subscriptions (no WhatsApp Cloud API, no Twilio, no third-party cloud hosting fees).

## 2. The 5-Minute Live Demonstration (Smallest Feasible Demo)

The owner can demonstrate the entire system to a doctor or clinic owner in 5 minutes using their personal mobile phone:

### Step 1: The Simulated Patient Inbound (Minute 1)
- The owner asks the doctor: *"Doctor, when someone finds your clinic on Google Maps at 9:30 PM, how does your clinic currently reply?"*
- Explain: *"Let me show you what a modern automated patient intake looks like from a patient's phone."*

### Step 2: Live Inbound Demonstration (Minutes 2–3)
- The owner shows a sample WhatsApp Business profile configured with after-hours away messaging:
  > *"Hello! Thank you for contacting our clinic. Our practice is currently closed (Hours: Mon-Sat 9 AM - 8 PM).*  
  > *To request an appointment for tomorrow, reply 1.*  
  > *For treatment fee information, reply 2.*  
  > *For acute dental emergencies, call our on-call line."*

### Step 3: Reception Quick-Reply Demonstration (Minutes 3–4)
- On desktop or phone, demonstrate typing shortcut `/fee` or `/hours`:
  - Instant expansion into structured clinic information.
  - Show how reception staff saves 10–15 minutes per inquiry.

### Step 4: The Shared Tracking Sheet (Minute 5)
- Open Google Sheet showing: inquiry date, patient name, inquiry topic, response time, and consultation outcome.
- Conclude: *"We configure this entire setup on your reception's phone (Setup duration: OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED), train your staff, and give you a weekly show-rate summary. You retain 100% control of your phone and data."*

## 4. Onboarding & Kickoff Protocol (Day 1)

When a clinic owner accepts the pilot:

1. **Intake Form:** Complete the 8 parameters from `owner/PILOT_DELIVERY_SPEC.md` (operating hours, top 5 FAQs, emergency number, booking rules).
2. **Staff Verification:** Confirm the reception mobile device has WhatsApp Business App installed.
3. **Template Approval:** Doctor reviews and signs off on greeting text, away message text, and quick-reply shortcuts.
4. **Calendar Connection:** Configure Cal.com or Google Calendar to doctor chair availability.
