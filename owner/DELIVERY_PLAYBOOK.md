## 2. Intake Questions for the Client (Clinic / Business)

Gather these during the 30-minute kickoff meeting with the business owner:

1. **Top 5 Inbound Inquiries:** What are the 5 questions prospective clients ask repeatedly before booking? (e.g., "What are consultation fees?", "Are appointments required?", "Do you handle dental implants/root canals?", "Where is clinic located?")
2. **Business Operating Hours:** Exact opening and closing times for weekdays, Saturdays, and Sundays.
3. **Emergency / After-Hours Procedure:** If a patient reaches out with acute pain or urgent need after hours, what is the exact emergency protocol?
4. **Appointment Booking Workflow:** Who manages the chair time? Can appointments be booked automatically into a designated Google Calendar, or must reception confirm?
5. **Fee Structure:** Standard consultation charge, payment methods accepted (UPI, Cash, Cards).
6. **DPDP Consent Confirmation:** Confirmation that the clinic obtains client consent before collecting mobile numbers for appointment reminders.

## 5. What the Owner Must NEVER Promise

To maintain absolute integrity and avoid statutory, platform, or legal liabilities:

1. ❌ **NEVER promise Google Business Profile Chat Automation:** Google Business Profile chat was permanently deprecated and shut down on July 31, 2024. Do not promise automated chat directly inside Google Maps or Search.
2. ❌ **NEVER promise 100% Conversion or Rupee Return on Investment:** Conversion depends on clinic location, doctor reputation, patient pricing, and clinical quality. Promise *response-time speed* and *zero missed inquiries*, never guaranteed patient revenue.
3. ❌ **NEVER send cold outbound unsolicited WhatsApp messages:** WhatsApp strictly penalizes spam. Only respond to inbound inquiries where the user contacted the business first.
4. ❌ **NEVER promise AI medical diagnosis or clinical advice:** The bot is strictly a front-desk triage and appointment scheduling tool. All clinical decisions belong exclusively to qualified medical practitioners.
5. ❌ **NEVER share patient contact details:** DPDP Act 2023 imposes heavy penalties for unauthorized data sharing. All data stays strictly in client's own app and private sheet.
