- **Offer ID:** `PLATFORM_SETUP`
- **Full Title:** AI Inbound Lead Conversion System — Day 0-5 Setup & Integration
- **Price:** ₹15,000 (Fixed server-side authoritative price)
- **Billing Model:** ONE_TIME
- **Delivery Time:** 5 Business Days
- **Target Audience:** Local service businesses, healthcare practices, clinics, dental centers, coaching institutes, and professional service firms in India.

### What the Buyer Gets (Deliverables)
1. **Custom WhatsApp Triage and FAQ Response Bot**
   - Automated greeting, qualification triage, and instant response under 2 minutes for inbound WhatsApp inquiries.
   - Customized answers to top 20 business FAQs (pricing, hours, location, appointment procedures).
2. **Google Business Profile (GBP) Instant Lead Capture**

3. **Multi-Touch Follow-Up Workflow**
   - 3-step automated follow-up sequence for inquiries that stop responding before booking.
4. **Direct Calendar Booking Integration**
   - Frictionless appointment scheduling link connected to practitioner or staff calendar.
5. **Live System Handover Report & Staff Training**
   - Step-by-step walkthrough documentation and verification test run confirming zero missed inquiries.

### Qualification Requirements
Before onboarding, the client must have:
- An active WhatsApp Business number.
- A claimed, verified Google Business Profile.
