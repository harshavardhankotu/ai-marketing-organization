## 2. Structured UTM Parameter Standard

To ensure immutable click provenance and prevent attribution contamination across analytics logs, construct external marketing links with the following parameter taxonomy:

```text
https://ai-marketing-organization.onrender.com/guides/best-wireless-audio-smartwatches-india?utm_source={source}&utm_medium={medium}&utm_campaign={campaign}&utm_content={content}
```

### Approved UTM Values

* **`utm_source`:** Must reflect the actual distribution channel:
  - `whatsapp`: Direct peer broadcast or community message
  - `telegram`: Broadcast channel or deal group
  - `twitter`: Public post or reply thread
  - `linkedin`: Professional article or post
  - `newsletter`: Email newsletter dispatch
  - `organic_search`: Organic indexing referral
* **`utm_medium`:**
  - `social`: Unpaid public social media share
  - `direct_share`: 1-to-1 messaging share
  - `community`: Group / forum distribution
  - `newsletter`: Structured email send
* **`utm_campaign`:**
  - `electronics_deals_2026`: Personal audio and wearables promotions
  - `student_travel_2026`: Student flight guidance dissemination
  - `sitefix_growth_2026`: Technical website audit acquisition
  - `practice_lead_capture_2026`: Dental/healthcare administrative capture
* **`utm_content`:**
  - `headline_a`: Hero copy variant
  - `bullet_breakdown`: Spec summary snippet
  - `cta_button`: Direct click-through anchor

## 4. Cuelinks Campaign Permission & Compliance Checklist

Before distributing any affiliate deal or product link, verify that the merchant campaign satisfies all 5 commercial constraints:

1. **`channel_id` Verification:** The approved Cuelinks Channel ID (`331585`) must be active and approved in the publisher console.
2. **`social_media_publishers_allowed`:** Confirm that the merchant campaign terms permit social media and messaging distribution (WhatsApp, Telegram, Twitter). If social publishers are restricted, traffic must land on the website first rather than outbound direct links.
3. **`deeplink_allowed`:** Verify that product-level deep links are supported. Never construct synthetic deep links for merchants that only allow root domain referrals.
4. **`sub_ids_allowed`:** Confirm sub-ID tracking (`subid` through `subid5`) is enabled to preserve click attribution and hourly window reconciliation.
5. **Zero Fake Urgency:** Never fabricate false countdown timers, invented discount percentages, or unbacked claim penalties (e.g. "₹5,000 fine"). State only verified MRP baselines and active merchant voucher terms.
