### 1.1 Five-Point Gold Buyer Safety Checklist
Before purchasing gold or silver coins online during Diwali/Dhanteras:
1. **Mandatory BIS Hallmark & HUID:** Every gold coin must carry the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) hallmark and a 6-digit alphanumeric HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification). Verify on the BIS Care App.
2. **Purity Standard:** 22 Karat equals 91.6% purity (marked `916`); 24 Karat equals 99.9% purity (marked `999`).
3. **Making Charges & Wastage:** Compare making charges across jewellers. Reputable online brand stores charge 1%–3% on coins compared to 8%–18% on ornamental jewellery.
4. **Tamper-Evident Packaging:** Ensure packaging is factory-sealed with an assay card certifying weight and purity.
5. **GST Invoice & Purity Breakdown:** Insist on an explicit invoice separating gold price per gram, making charges, and 3% GST.
