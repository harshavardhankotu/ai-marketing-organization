# Rule: Revenue Integrity and Strict Data Classification

## Core Principle
Marketing without verified business revenue is vanity. However, claiming synthetic or mocked revenue as genuine production revenue is fraud. The system must rigorously track and separate all monetary data.

## Invariant Rules
1. **Strict Tri-State Mode Isolation**:
   - Every transaction, conversion, lead, and revenue attribution record MUST be explicitly tagged with its execution mode:
     - `REAL`: Ingested from real production payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe, Cashfree, bank feeds, clinic/business billing).
     - `TEST`: Generated during automated integration tests or sandbox verification with test credentials.
     - `SIMULATED`: Generated by synthetic market simulation, load testing, or demonstration models.
2. **Zero Fake Real Revenue**: Synthetic or simulated numbers must NEVER be aggregated into `REAL` revenue metrics or reported as actual business earnings.
3. **Double-Entry Attribution**: Revenue must trace through the deterministic customer lifecycle:
   `Visitor → Session → Lead → Qualified Lead → Opportunity → Customer → Transaction → Attribution → Revenue`.
4. **AI Unit Economics**: Calculate true net efficiency by logging AI API token costs (prompt + completion + thinking) against attributed revenue:
   - `AI Cost per Qualified Lead`
   - `AI Cost per Paying Customer`
   - `AI Cost to Attributed Revenue Ratio`
