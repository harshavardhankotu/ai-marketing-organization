## 1. Google AdSense (Display Advertising on Web Content)

- **Evaluation Tag:** `LATER`
- **Official Requirements:**
  - Owner must be 18+ years old (Source: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724).
  - Domain must host unique, high-quality, original content with smooth user navigation.
  - Full compliance with Google Publisher Policies & Program Policies (https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182).
  - Direct ownership of domain with access to edit HTML `<head>` tag for AdSense snippet.
- **Costs:**
  - ₹0 application or platform fee. (Custom domain renewal costs ~₹800–1,200/year if not already owned).
- **Time to Eligibility:**
  - 2 to 4 weeks after website is indexed with 20–30 comprehensive original articles.
- **Traffic Needed:**
  - Technically no minimum pageview threshold to apply, but practically requires 10,000+ organic monthly pageviews to generate meaningful ad impressions.
- **Data Sources & Usage Rights:**
  - Own original editorial content; zero AI-scraped or uncurated syndicated content (violates Google Spam Policies).
- **Policy Risks:**
  - Immediate account suspension for invalid traffic (self-clicks, bot traffic). Ads.txt compliance mandatory.
- **Fit with System Rules:**
  - Compatible with free hosting on Firebase/Render. However, requires substantial organic search traffic that does not exist today. Mark: `LATER`.
- **Projected Income:** `UNKNOWN`

## 2. Google AdMob (In-App Mobile Advertising)

- **Evaluation Tag:** `NOT_FREE`
- **Official Requirements:**
  - Must distribute a native Android (APK/AAB) or iOS app via official app stores.
  - Active Google Play Console account or Apple Developer Program membership.
  - App must comply with Google Publisher Policies (https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).
- **Costs:**
  - Google Play Console Developer Registration: **$25 USD (one-time fee)** (~₹2,100).
  - Apple Developer Program: **$99 USD/year** (~₹8,300/year).
  - Therefore this lane is **NOT FREE**.
- **Time to Eligibility:**
  - 1 to 3 months (app development, closed testing requirements: 20 testers for 14 days on Google Play, and store review).
- **Traffic Needed:**
  - 1,000+ daily active users (DAU) to register baseline impressions.
- **Data Sources & Usage Rights:**
  - Proprietary app code and licensed user assets.
- **Policy Risks:**
  - Google Play policy compliance rejections; ad placement policy violations (accidental clicks).
- **Fit with System Rules:**
  - Violates the strict "Free plans only / Zero rupee expenses" rule due to the mandatory $25 developer registration fee. Mark: `NOT_FREE`.
- **Projected Income:** `UNKNOWN`

## 3. YouTube Monetization (YouTube Partner Program)

- **Evaluation Tag:** `LATER`
- **Official Requirements:**
  - Compliance with YouTube Channel Monetization Policies (Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851).
  - 2-Step Verification enabled; zero active Community Guidelines strikes.
  - **Tier 1 (Fan Funding / Super Chat):** 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 public watch hours (or 3M Shorts views).
  - **Tier 2 (Ad Revenue Sharing):** 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 public watch hours in past 365 days (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days).
- **Costs:**
  - ₹0 platform cost. Free to create channel and upload content.
- **Time to Eligibility:**
  - Typically 6 to 12 months of consistent weekly video publishing.
- **Traffic Needed:**
  - 4,000 watch hours (~240,000 minutes of watched video) or 10,000,000 Shorts views.
- **Data Sources & Usage Rights:**
  - 100% original video footage and royalty-free audio (YouTube Audio Library). Synthetic/AI-voice videos face heavy scrutiny under "repetitious/reused content" guidelines.
- **Policy Risks:**
  - Copyright strikes, reused content demonetization, sudden algorithm shifts.
- **Fit with System Rules:**
  - Free to operate, but high time investment before reaching eligibility threshold. Mark: `LATER`.
- **Projected Income:** `UNKNOWN`

## 4. Fuel & Mileage Utility Tools (Web-Based Calculators)

- **Evaluation Tag:** `LATER`
- **Official Requirements:**
  - Static or lightweight web application hosted on Firebase / Cloudflare Pages.
  - Interactive fuel cost calculators, trip expense splitters, and mileage comparison widgets.
- **Costs:**
  - ₹0 hosting on Firebase Hosting free tier or Cloudflare Pages.
  - Free public data from state petroleum portals (e.g. Indian Oil / BPCL daily price publications).
- **Time to Eligibility:**
  - Tool can be launched in 3 to 5 days; monetization eligibility (via AdSense or sponsored local garage listings) requires 2 to 3 months of SEO indexing.
- **Traffic Needed:**
  - 25,000+ monthly visits to attract local automotive or insurance sponsors.
- **Data Sources & Usage Rights:**
  - Daily city-wise retail selling price (RSP) published publicly under open government data guidelines.
- **Policy Risks:**
  - Low policy risk. Calculator utility has high programmatic utility.
- **Fit with System Rules:**
  - Free tools, policy safe, fits static Firebase deployment. Requires audience accumulation. Mark: `LATER`.
- **Projected Income:** `UNKNOWN`

## 5. Free-Resource Discovery (Directory of Schemes & Subsidies)

- **Evaluation Tag:** `LATER`
- **Official Requirements:**
  - Curated, searchable directory of central & state government welfare schemes (e.g. MSME credit schemes, startup seed funding, student educational grants).
  - Official public data source: myScheme portal (https://www.myscheme.gov.in) and National Portal of India (https://www.india.gov.in).
- **Costs:**
  - ₹0 hosting on Firebase Hosting / Cloudflare D1 free tier.
- **Time to Eligibility:**
  - 1 to 2 months for indexing and user discovery.
- **Traffic Needed:**
  - 15,000+ monthly searches.
- **Data Sources & Usage Rights:**
  - Public domain government scheme guidelines and notifications. Must provide clear disclaimers that the site is an independent informative portal and not a government entity.
- **Policy Risks:**
  - Must strictly avoid misrepresenting as an official government agency (prohibited by IT Rules and Google Ads policies). No collection of Aadhaar or sensitive PII.
- **Fit with System Rules:**
  - Free to build and host. Mark: `LATER`.
- **Projected Income:** `UNKNOWN`

## 6. Amazon Associates & Multi-Network Affiliate Lanes

- **Evaluation Tag:** `LATER` (Blocked until statutory owner facts exist)
- **Official Requirements:**
  - Amazon Associates India Operating Agreement (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operatingagreement).
  - Must submit active, compliant website with original content and prominent affiliate disclosure.
  - **180-Day Probation Rule:** Must generate at least 3 qualifying sales within 180 days of application, or account is terminated.
  - Statutory Owner Facts must be provided by the human owner: `ASSOCIATES_APPLICATION_DATE`, `ASSOCIATES_LISTED_SITE_URL`, `ASSOCIATES_AGREEMENT_READ`, `PUBLIC_SITE_NAME`, `PUBLIC_AUTHOR_NAME`, `PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL`.
- **Costs:**
  - ₹0 signup and platform cost.
- **Time to Eligibility:**
  - Immediate provisional account upon application; full review after driving 3 sales.
- **Traffic Needed:**
  - 500+ targeted buyer clicks per month to reliably achieve 3 conversions.
- **Data Sources & Usage Rights:**
  - Amazon Product Advertising API (PA-API) requires active approved sales. In provisional mode, manual search links with proper associate tags are permitted. Scraping amazon.* pages is strictly banned.
- **Policy Risks:**
  - Termination for failure to achieve 3 sales within 180 days; prohibition against offline linking or sending links via personal WhatsApp/email newsletters.
- **Fit with System Rules:**
  - Fully implemented in codebase (automatic offer lane, compliance linter, mask tags). Currently gated by missing statutory owner variables. Mark: `LATER`.
- **Projected Income:** `UNKNOWN`
